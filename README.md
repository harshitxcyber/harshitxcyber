## About

I am an **MBA Business Analytics candidate at Anurag University, Hyderabad**, focused on transforming complex datasets into reliable analytical systems, interactive BI experiences, and decision-ready business intelligence.

My work combines **Python, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, Advanced Excel, statistical analysis, relational data modelling, automation, and KPI engineering**. I approach analytics with an engineering mindset: clean data pipelines, structured schemas, reusable logic, validated metrics, maintainable dashboards, and measurable business outcomes.

I have worked with datasets exceeding **99,999 records**, designed relational databases using **3NF normalisation**, built automated reporting workflows, developed executive KPI dashboards, and translated behavioural and operational data into actionable recommendations.

My experience also includes applying analytics in digital platforms and government communication environments, where data-backed strategies contributed to measurable improvements in reporting efficiency, digital engagement, and marketing performance.

Alongside business intelligence, I am strengthening my foundations in **AI/ML, statistical modelling, analytics engineering, and product engineering**. My long-term direction is to build intelligent data products where analytics, software engineering principles, machine learning, and business strategy operate as one system.

### Product Engineering Mindset

- Build systems that solve measurable business problems.
- Design reliable data models before designing dashboards.
- Automate repetitive analytical workflows.
- Prioritise data validation, documentation, scalability, and maintainability.
- Translate technical outputs into clear business decisions.
- Develop analytics products around users, outcomes, and measurable impact.

### Open To

`Data Analyst` · `Business Analyst` · `BI Analyst` · `Analytics Internships` · `Analytics Engineering` · `AI/ML Analytics` · `Data Product Projects` · `Open Source Collaboration`
